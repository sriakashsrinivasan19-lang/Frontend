Guidelines for Delivary Partners

Guidelines for online delivery partners typically mandate strict onboarding, including government-issued ID and background checks, and set expectations for app usage, hygiene, and safe navigation. Partners must maintain high acceptance rates, follow tamper-evident packaging protocols, and adhere to traffic and safety laws. Platforms in India often require partners to complete specific training modules. For instance, you can review the comprehensive training guidelines and expectations directly on the Swiggy Delivery Partner FAQs or explore driver resources via the Uber Driver platform.
